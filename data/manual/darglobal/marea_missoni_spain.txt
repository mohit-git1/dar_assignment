TITLE: Marea, Interiors by Missoni | Luxury Residences in Cortesin, Costa del Sol, Spain
Marea by Missoni is an ultra-exclusive gated residential development by DarGlobal situated within Finca Cortesin in Casares, Costa del Sol, Spain. Overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, the development features interior design by the legendary Italian luxury fashion house Missoni.

Location:
- Finca Cortesin, Casares / Costa del Sol, Spain (between Marbella and Sotogrande).
- Set within a world-renowned luxury resort known for its 5-star hotel, Michelin-starred dining, and an award-winning championship golf course that hosted the Solheim Cup and Volvo World Match Play Championship.

Architecture and Missoni Interior Design:
- Inspired by the coastal Mediterranean setting, the architecture combines modern minimalist lines with Missoni's signature vibrant prints, natural textures, and artistic geometry.
- Floor-to-ceiling glass windows capture uninterrupted views of the Mediterranean coastline, the Rock of Gibraltar, and the North African mountain ranges.

Residences & Floorplans:
- 2, 3, and 4-bedroom luxury residences and garden apartments.
- Large private terraces, open-plan gourmet kitchens with high-end appliances, and marble-clad bathrooms.

Resort Amenities:
- Private infinity pool with views over the Mediterranean and golf fairways
- State-of-the-art gym, wellness spa, and tennis / padel courts
- Private gardens and quiet reflection zones
- 24/7 security with gated perimeter control and concierge services
- Preferred access to Finca Cortesin golf club, beach club, and spa facilities

Investment and Lifestyle:
- Prime second-home investment in Europe's most prestigious golfing destination.
