TITLE: Urban Oasis by Missoni | Waterfront Branded Living on Dubai Canal
Urban Oasis is a signature luxury residential development by DarGlobal situated right on the banks of the Dubai Water Canal in Business Bay, Dubai. The tower features interiors exclusively designed by iconic Italian fashion house Missoni, marking the fashion brand's prestigious second residential project in the world and its first in the Middle East.

Design and Missoni Aesthetic:
- Interiors by Missoni: Renowned for iconic zigzag motifs, rich color palettes, and handcrafted textiles, Missoni's creative touch infuses the entire tower, from the grand lobby to individual apartments and penthouses.
- Prime Canal Frontage: Front-row positioning on the Dubai Water Canal, offering water views, boardwalk strolls, and proximity to Downtown Dubai.

Unit Configurations:
- Studio apartments, 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom luxury residences, and exclusive full-floor penthouses.
- Every residence features expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, private balconies with canal and skyline views, and custom Missoni Home fixtures and furnishings.

Lifestyle & Resort Amenities:
- Waterfront infinity pool directly overlooking the Dubai Canal
- Fully equipped gym and aquatic workout zone
- Landscaped leisure deck with cabanas and lounge areas
- Dedicated 24/7 concierge, private parking, and high-speed elevators
- Immediate access to canal boat taxis and promenade dining

Strategic Value:
- High rental liquidity in Business Bay driven by luxury brand recognition (Missoni).
- Ideal for second-home buyers and short-term luxury rentals.
- Purchases of AED 2M and above qualify international buyers for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa.
