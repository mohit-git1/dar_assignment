TITLE: DarGlobal Residential Projects | Luxury Branded Second Homes Portfolio
DarGlobal is a premier international real estate developer listed on the London Stock Exchange (LSE: DAR). DarGlobal specializes in high-end luxury residential developments, branded second homes, and vacation residences in iconic global destinations.

Overview of DarGlobal's Key Residential Projects:
1. Urban Oasis by Missoni (Dubai, United Arab Emirates): Located on the prestigious Dubai Water Canal, Urban Oasis features waterfront 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom apartments and penthouses with bespoke interiors designed by renowned Italian fashion house Missoni.
2. DaVinci Tower by Pagani (Downtown Dubai, UAE): An ultra-luxury residential skyscraper featuring geometric aesthetics and automotive-inspired interiors designed by Horacio Pagani. Overlooks Burj Khalifa and the Dubai Canal.
3. DG1 Living (Dubai Canal, Business Bay, UAE): Modern luxury residences designed by Gensler architects offering contemporary 1, 2, and 3-bedroom residences with sweeping views of the canal and Downtown Dubai skyline.
4. W Residences Dubai - Downtown (Dubai, UAE): Exclusive branded residences managed by Marriott International's luxury W Hotels brand, providing residents with 5-star hospitality, hotel-style amenities, and direct views of Burj Khalifa.
5. AIDA (Muscat, Oman): Oceanfront clifftop community featuring luxury villas, mansions, and residences surrounding the Trump International Golf Club Oman.
6. Les Vagues by Elie Saab (Qetaifan Island North, Qatar): Premium beachfront residential development featuring 1, 2, and 3-bedroom luxury apartments with interiors styled by Lebanese haute couture designer Elie Saab.
7. Tierra Viva (Benahavis, Costa del Sol, Spain): Exclusive gated enclave of ultra-luxury villas located near Marbella, featuring architectural concepts and interiors directly inspired by Automobili Lamborghini.

DarGlobal projects are distinguished by strategic prime locations, partnerships with global luxury brands, high capital appreciation potential, and bespoke lifestyle amenities.
