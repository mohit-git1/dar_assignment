TITLE: The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin | Beachfront Residences on Al Marjan Island RAK
The Astera is an ultra-luxury beachfront residential project developed by DarGlobal on Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah (RAK), UAE. Featuring bespoke interiors created by celebrated British ultra-luxury automotive brand Aston Martin, The Astera represents the marque's first beachfront residential development in the world.

Location & Wynn Resort Synergy:
- Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah: An archipelago of four man-made coral-shaped islands in the Arabian Gulf, becoming a world-class tourism, luxury, and gaming destination.
- Direct Neighbor to Wynn Al Marjan Island: Situated in close proximity to the landmark multi-billion dollar Wynn Resort (the UAE's first integrated gaming resort), creating exceptional capital appreciation and short-term hospitality rental yield potential.

Architecture and Aston Martin Interior Craftsmanship:
- The tower combines aerodynamic exterior lines with British automotive luxury.
- Interiors feature signature Aston Martin leather upholstery, fine wood veneers, metallic accents, and bespoke craftsmanship mirroring the cockpits of Aston Martin sports cars.

Residences and Amenities:
- Beachfront 1, 2, and 3-bedroom residences, seaside villas, and the ultra-exclusive "Top of the Astera" signature sky penthouse collection.
- Private sandy beach with direct Arabian Gulf access and private cabanas
- Infinity swimming pool elevated over the turquoise waters
- Aston Martin private resident cinema screening room
- State-of-the-art wellness club, luxury spa, and outdoor yoga lawn
- 24/7 concierge, valet, and yacht mooring services

Residency and Tax Benefits:
- Ras Al Khaimah offers a favorable tax environment (0% personal tax, 0% capital gains tax).
- Qualifying purchases grant eligibility for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa.
