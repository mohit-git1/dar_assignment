TITLE: AIDA 360 by DarGlobal | Luxury Gated Community & Trump International Golf Club Oman
AIDA is an extraordinary master-planned coastal destination developed by DarGlobal, perched over 130 meters above the shores of the Gulf of Oman in Yiti, Muscat. Spanning over 3.5 million square meters of pristine clifftop terrain, AIDA offers an unmatched blend of luxury, natural beauty, and world-class architectural design.

Key Amenities at Aida 360:
- Trump International Golf Club Oman: A premier 18-hole championship golf course offering spectacular clifftop holes overlooking the sea, state-of-the-art golf academy, and luxury clubhouse.
- Clifftop Promenade & Viewing Decks: Scenic pedestrian trails and lookouts providing 360-degree panoramic views of the ocean, mountains, and coastal cliffs.
- Cliff-Hanging Nightlife & Dining: World-class signature restaurants, lounges, and social clubs situated on dramatic canyon edges.
- Wellness Centers & Spas: Holistic wellness retreats, fitness studios, thermal pools, and tranquil relaxation zones.
- Outdoor Recreation: Extensive hiking and running trails, mountain biking paths, and curated desert nature walks.
- Private Swimming Pools & Infinity Pools: Clifftop infinity pools with horizon sea views.
- 5-Star Boutique Hotels & Hospitality: Ultra-luxury hospitality accommodations and bespoke concierge services for residents and international guests.

Residential Offerings:
AIDA features an exclusive collection of luxury 3-bedroom, 4-bedroom, 5-bedroom, 6-bedroom, and 7-bedroom villas, cliffside ocean mansions, and designer branded apartments. Every residence is meticulously crafted with premium materials, open-concept indoor-outdoor living spaces, private gardens, and floor-to-ceiling windows to capture the dramatic ocean and canyon vistas.
