TITLE: Les Vagues by Elie Saab | Beachfront Residences on Qetaifan Island North Qatar
Les Vagues is a luxury beachfront residential project by DarGlobal located on Qetaifan Island North in Lusail / Doha, Qatar. Featuring haute couture interior styling by iconic Lebanese fashion designer Elie Saab, Les Vagues brings world-class architectural elegance to the shores of the Arabian Gulf.

Location - Qetaifan Island North:
- Situated in Lusail, Qatar's visionary smart city and premier entertainment capital.
- Qetaifan Island North is renowned as Qatar's entertainment hub, featuring the Meryal waterpark, luxury boutique hotels, private beaches, linear parks, and a world-class marina.

Elie Saab Interior Aesthetic & Architecture:
- Sleek, modern sea-facing architecture with curvilinear balconies reflecting the waves of the sea (hence the name "Les Vagues").
- Interiors designed by Elie Saab, emphasizing warm neutral tones, bronze and brass accents, custom Elie Saab Maison furnishings, and floor-to-ceiling glass.

Residential Features & Amenities:
- 1, 2, and 3-bedroom luxury waterfront apartments with open sea and beach views.
- Private sandy beach with direct water access
- Infinity-edge swimming pool overlooking the coastline
- State-of-the-art fitness center with top-tier training equipment
- Landscaped community courtyard, private garden lounges, and children's play area
- 24/7 concierge, private parking, and high-security access

Investment in Qatar:
- Freehold property ownership for international investors.
- Direct eligibility for Qatar permanent residency with healthcare and education benefits for property owners.
- Strong rental yields in Lusail driven by ongoing international tourism and economic expansion.
