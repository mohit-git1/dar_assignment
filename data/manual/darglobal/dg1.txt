TITLE: DG1 Living by DarGlobal | Luxury Waterfront Apartments in Business Bay Dubai

Designed by world-renowned architecture firm Gensler, DG1 embodies contemporary architectural distinction, featuring dynamic sculpted façades that maximize panoramic canal and skyline vistas.

Key Project Highlights:
- Developer: DarGlobal (LSE: DAR)
- Architecture: Gensler
- Location: Business Bay, on the banks of Dubai Water Canal, Dubai, UAE
- Property Types: Premium 1, 2, and 3-bedroom luxury apartments
- Views: Uninterrupted views of the Dubai Water Canal, Downtown Dubai skyline, and Burj Khalifa
