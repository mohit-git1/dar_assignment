TITLE: Tierra Viva by Automobili Lamborghini & DarGlobal | Luxury Villas in Benahavís Spain
Tierra Viva is an ultra-exclusive gated residential community of 53 architectural masterpiece villas developed by DarGlobal in Benahavís, Costa del Sol, Spain. This marks Automobili Lamborghini’s first residential development in Europe, featuring sharp lines, bold aesthetics, and supercar-inspired design philosophies.

Location:
- Benahavís, Costa del Sol, Andalusia, Spain (just minutes from Marbella and Puerto Banús).
- Nestled on a private hillside overlooking the Mediterranean Sea, offering panoramic coastal views and serene exclusivity while remaining within easy reach of world-class marinas, dining, and golf clubs.

Architectural Villa Collections:
1. Diamante Villas (6 Bedrooms): The most prestigious multi-level mansions, featuring expansive rooftop terraces, private infinity pools, lush gardens, and extensive entertainment zones.
2. Zafiro Villas (5 Bedrooms): Magnificent multi-tier hillside villas with panoramic sea views, wine cellars, private home gyms, and spa facilities.
3. Esmeralda Villas (4 Bedrooms): Elegant, light-filled modern villas with private infinity pools, manicured Mediterranean gardens, and seamless indoor-outdoor living spaces.

Lamborghini Design Details & Features:
- Geometric hexagonal motifs, sharp lines, and aerodynamic exterior architecture evoking Lamborghini supercars like the Aventador and Revuelto.
- High-grade Italian finishes, bespoke luxury fixtures, and custom marble.
- Direct elevator access to all levels in every villa, private multi-car garages, and state-of-the-art smart home integration.
- Gated community with 24/7 private security, surveillance, and concierge services.

Investment and Lifestyle:
- A flagship European luxury property investment blending European lifestyle appeal with high capital retention and scarcity value.
