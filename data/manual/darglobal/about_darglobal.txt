TITLE: About DarGlobal | Difference Between DarGlobal and Wasalt
About DarGlobal:
DarGlobal (Dar Global PLC) is an international real estate developer listed on the London Stock Exchange (LSE: DAR). Founded as the international development division of Dar Al Arkan (a major Saudi Arabian real estate enterprise), DarGlobal focuses exclusively on creating ultra-luxury branded residences, holiday homes, and second-home developments in top international tourist and investment hubs. DarGlobal operates globally with premier projects in the United Arab Emirates (Dubai), Oman, Qatar, Spain (Costa del Sol), Bosnia, and the United Kingdom. It is famous for exclusive co-branding agreements with luxury houses including Missoni, Pagani, Elie Saab, Automobili Lamborghini, and the Trump Organization.

Difference Between DarGlobal and Wasalt:
- Business Model & Nature of Platform:
  * DarGlobal is an international real estate developer and asset creator. It designs, develops, builds, and sells its own exclusive luxury residential and mixed-use projects in global destinations.
  * Wasalt (wasalt.sa) is a comprehensive Saudi Arabian property marketplace and digital portal. Wasalt is an online real estate platform that lists thousands of properties for sale, rent, and auction across Saudi Arabia (such as Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, and Al Khobar).
- Geographic Scope:
  * DarGlobal develops international properties outside Saudi Arabia (Dubai, Oman, Spain, Qatar, London).
  * Wasalt is primarily focused on the domestic real estate market within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
- Audience & Listings:
  * DarGlobal serves global affluent investors and second-home buyers seeking branded luxury villas, penthouses, and golf estates.
  * Wasalt caters to domestic homebuyers, renters, commercial tenants, real estate brokers, and local certified developers across Saudi Arabia looking to buy, rent, or auction apartments, villas, and land.
