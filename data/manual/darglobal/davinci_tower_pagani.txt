TITLE: Da Vinci Tower by Pagani & DarGlobal | Ultra-Luxury Residences in Downtown Dubai
Da Vinci Tower (also known as DaVinci Tower by Pagani) is an ultra-exclusive residential landmark developed by DarGlobal in Downtown Dubai, featuring bespoke interior design and handcrafted finishes by Italian hypercar legend Pagani Automobili. 

Architecture and Pagani Automotive Design:
- Design Inspiration: Directly inspired by Horacio Pagani's philosophy of Art and Science (Arte e Scienza), echoing the aerodynamic curves and carbon-fiber detailing of Pagani hypercars like the Zonda and Huayra.
- Unique Kinetic Elements: The tower features a suspended geometric sphere and aerodynamic curves with bespoke carbon fiber, brushed aluminum, and premium Italian leather interior finishes.
- Location: Downtown Dubai / Dubai Water Canal, offering frontline views of Burj Khalifa, Downtown Dubai, and the Arabian Gulf.

Residential Units and The Pagani Penthouse:
- 2, 3, and 4-bedroom ultra-luxury apartments and sky duplexes.
- The Pagani Penthouse (One of One): An ultra-rare signature crown jewel penthouse occupying the uppermost floors with private elevator access, private 360-degree infinity pool, custom Pagani furnishings, and bespoke artisan craftwork.

Exclusive Resident Amenities:
- Rooftop infinity pool with 360-degree panoramic skyline and Burj Khalifa views
- Pagani-themed wellness sanctuary, sauna, steam rooms, and modern gym
- Private resident screening room and cinema lounge
- 24/7 dedicated white-glove concierge, private chauffeur, valet parking, and high-security access controls

Investment and Residency:
- Positioned as a true collectible asset for high-net-worth global investors and automotive connoisseurs.
- Fully eligible for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa for purchases meeting or exceeding AED 2,000,000.
