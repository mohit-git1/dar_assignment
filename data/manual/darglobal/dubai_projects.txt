TITLE: DarGlobal Dubai Residential Properties | Flats, Waterfront Towers and Penthouses
DarGlobal's flagship United Arab Emirates portfolio features world-class residential towers and luxury flats situated along the Dubai Water Canal and Downtown Dubai.

Major Developments in Dubai:
- Urban Oasis by Missoni: Located in Business Bay along the waterfront of the Dubai Canal. Offers luxury 1, 2, 3-bedroom waterfront flats and exclusive 4-bedroom penthouses with private elevators and Missoni Home interiors. Starting prices during developer launch ranged from approximately AED 1,200,000 for 1-bedroom apartments to higher tiers for waterfront penthouses.
- DaVinci Tower by Pagani: Located directly on the Dubai Canal overlooking Burj Khalifa. Ultra-luxury branded apartments featuring bespoke Pagani materials, private carbon-fiber detailing, and sweeping canal views.
- DG1 Living: Located at Business Bay / Dubai Canal, featuring contemporary architectural design and luxury apartments.
- W Residences Dubai - Downtown: Branded hotel-serviced apartments offering panoramic views of the Burj Khalifa and Downtown Dubai.

Note Regarding Dubai Marina vs Dubai Canal:
DarGlobal's primary developments in Dubai are concentrated along the Dubai Water Canal, Business Bay, and Downtown Dubai. DarGlobal is a developer of new landmark projects and does not operate an open brokerage exchange for general secondary market flats in Dubai Marina. Consequently, verified pricing for general resale flats in Dubai Marina is not included in DarGlobal's developer catalog; buyers looking for secondary market apartments in Dubai Marina typically consult third-party UAE broker portals, whereas DarGlobal provides off-plan and newly delivered branded residences in its own towers.
